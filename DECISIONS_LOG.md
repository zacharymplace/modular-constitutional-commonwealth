## 2026-05-12 — Lock Party Power Roles

Decision:
The system will not enforce exactly three parties. It will lock five power roles:

1. Governing Power
2. Opposition Power
3. Plurality Power
4. Emergent Power
5. Stewardship Power

Rationale:
This prevents binary lockup without creating a fixed party cartel.

Status:
Locked.
