# The Modular Constitutional Commonwealth
## A Public Manifesto

A legitimate government should never become too powerful to question, too complex to understand, too captured to reform, or too rigid to improve.

The Modular Constitutional Commonwealth begins with a simple promise:

> The people must always be able to correct their government.

The goal is not bigger government or smaller government.

The goal is governable government:

- a government that can act, but can be checked;
- a government that can learn, but cannot secretly rule;
- a government that can use expertise, but remains democratic;
- a government that can plan for the future, but cannot abandon people now;
- a government that can respond to crisis, but must return to normal law;
- a government that can be complex internally, but clear to the public;
- a government that can be improved without being dismantled.

Shortest statement:

> Make democracy hard to kill.  
> Make policy easy to improve.  
> Make power impossible to hide.
