# Glossary

## Democratic Kernel
The protected constitutional foundation: rights, elections, courts, rule of law, peaceful transfer, and public ability to challenge government.

## Stewardship Power
Nonpartisan authority that protects democratic machinery without governing in place of elected officials.

## Public Accountability Rule
Every public power must have a visible owner, legal basis, appeal path, audit path, and review or expiration rule.

## No Hidden Sovereign
No algorithm, donor network, party machine, emergency body, expert office, vendor, or foreign actor may exercise public power without legal basis, public accountability, and review.
