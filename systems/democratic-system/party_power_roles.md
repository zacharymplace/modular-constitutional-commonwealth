# Party Power Roles

The Commonwealth does not lock in exactly three parties. It locks in democratic power roles filled by voter support, coalition outcomes, and constitutional compliance.

## Roles

1. Governing Power — governs
2. Opposition Power — audits
3. Plurality Power — broadens
4. Emergent Power — enters
5. Stewardship Power — protects the machinery

## Core Rule

Parties compete for power. They do not own the state.
